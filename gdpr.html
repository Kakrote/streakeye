GDPR

Our app comes with all the elements that are required to get a neat and ingenious design.

GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) sets strict rules for businesses that collect, store and process personal data of EU citizens. This new regulation is intended to affect organizations around the world that are involved in the collection and processing of personal data of EU citizens.

Thanks to the GDPR, citizens (customers) from the European Union can now exercise more control over their data and decide to what extent businesses can store and manage it.

The right to erasure

Compliance with the GDPR which states that individuals have the right to request the controller (company that has control over your data) to permanently delete their personal data. We have a system in place and make sure to respond to requests within a short period of time. Recipients are notified when we delete their data.

Data reduction
